### CHEM-000105: Optimal Lieb–Oxford constant
- **Subfield:** Theoretical and computational chemistry
- **Open:** Determine the smallest universal constant bounding the indirect Coulomb energy by the integral of the electron density to the four-thirds power. Distinguish the unrestricted bound from exchange-only bounds and bounds for restricted physical states.
- **Status:** Open since the 1981 inequality. Lewin, Lieb and Seiringer’s 2022 proof gives an upper bound of approximately 1.58; the reported lower bound exceeds 1.44.
- **Type:** theory
- **Why:** A sharper exact constraint would improve the mathematical foundation of exchange–correlation approximations.
- **Key works:** M. Lewin, E. H. Lieb and R. Seiringer (2022), *Improved Lieb–Oxford bound on the indirect and exchange energies*, [arXiv:2203.12473](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.12473), DOI: 10.1007/s11005-022-01584-5.
- **Related:** Mathematics: sharp constants in functional inequalities. Mathematical physics: crystallization of the classical electron gas.
